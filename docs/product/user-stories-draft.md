# User stories — discussion draft

**Status:** Draft for agreement. This file is deliberately not committed or pushed.

## Product principles

- The candidate remains in control: the product recommends and prepares; it does not apply or send outreach without explicit approval.
- A job offer always links to its original source.
- Match scores explain relevance; they do not claim to predict hiring.
- A candidate's CVs, applications, contacts, and connected Gmail account are private to that candidate.

## Identity and onboarding

### US-01 — Create an account

As a job seeker, I want to create and verify an account so that my search data is private and available when I return.

### US-02 — Sign in securely

As a job seeker, I want to sign in and stay signed in safely so that I can continue my search without exposing my account.

### US-03 — Set up my search profile

As a job seeker, I want to add my name, contact details, languages, target roles, and work preferences so that recommendations reflect my real situation.

### US-04 — Choose target countries

As a job seeker, I want to select and prioritise countries, including my work-authorisation situation, so that I can search across borders intentionally.

## CVs and candidate information

### US-05 — Upload and manage a CV

As a job seeker, I want to upload and name my CV so that I can reuse it for relevant applications.

### US-06 — Preserve CV history

As a job seeker, I want my original CV to remain unchanged when I create a tailored version so that I never lose my source document.

### US-07 — Tailor a CV for an opportunity

As a job seeker, I want to create a copy of a CV for a selected job offer so that I can adapt it while keeping a clear link to the version used.

## Job discovery

### US-08 — See relevant job offers

As a job seeker, I want to browse job offers matching my profile, target roles, and countries so that I can focus on worthwhile opportunities.

### US-09 — Understand a match

As a job seeker, I want to see a score and clear reasons for a job match so that I can judge it rather than blindly trust it.

### US-10 — Open the original job posting

As a job seeker, I want to open the original posting from every offer so that I can verify the details and apply through the correct channel.

### US-11 — Save or dismiss an offer

As a job seeker, I want to save promising offers and dismiss irrelevant ones so that recommendations and my workspace stay useful.

### US-12 — Search and filter offers

As a job seeker, I want to filter offers by country, location, remote policy, role, and date so that I can review the right opportunities quickly.

## Application management

### US-13 — Create an application from a job offer

As a job seeker, I want to turn a saved job offer into an application so that its source, job details, and chosen CV version are recorded together.

### US-14 — Add a manual application

As a job seeker, I want to record an application I found outside the product so that the tracker remains my complete source of truth.

### US-15 — Track application progress

As a job seeker, I want to update an application's status and see its history so that I know where every opportunity stands.

### US-16 — Keep a historical snapshot

As a job seeker, I want each application to retain the job title, company, URL, and CV version used at the time so that historical records remain accurate after an offer changes or disappears.

### US-17 — Manage contacts and follow-ups

As a job seeker, I want to attach contacts and reminders to an application so that I do not forget a recruiter, referral, or follow-up date.

### US-18 — Record interviews

As a job seeker, I want to record upcoming interviews and notes so that preparation and the application timeline stay in one place.

## Outreach and Gmail

### US-19 — Connect Gmail

As a job seeker, I want to connect my Gmail account explicitly so that I can create personal outreach drafts from an application.

### US-20 — Review a personalised outreach draft

As a job seeker, I want to review and edit a draft based only on my verified profile, selected CV version, and the actual job context so that I stay responsible for what is said in my name.

### US-21 — Send outreach after final approval

As a job seeker, I want to review a Gmail draft and explicitly confirm “Send now” before the product sends it so that no outreach is sent without my final approval.

The product never sends email automatically. It may send only after this explicit final confirmation.

## Privacy and control

### US-22 — Access only my data

As a job seeker, I want only my account to access my CVs, applications, contacts, and Gmail connection so that my private job search remains private.

### US-23 — Remove my data

As a job seeker, I want to delete a CV, Gmail connection, or account so that I can control personal data I no longer want stored.

## Not in the first agreed scope

- Automated application submission.
- Automated email sending.
- Community posts, direct messages, referrals, or social profiles.
- AI provider choice and AI-generated content beyond the approved-draft flow.
- Mobile client.
